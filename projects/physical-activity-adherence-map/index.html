Physical Activity Adherence — Digital Intervention Micro Map

What mechanisms do digital interventions use to help adults increase or sustain physical activity?

A descriptive convenience map of 10 selected studies. It is not a systematic review, meta-analysis, or cross-study effectiveness ranking.

Working model

measurement → personal feedback → goal / reference → prompt / support → repeat

The emerging question is less “does the intervention contain tracking?” and more “what does the feedback loop do after measurement, and does that remain behaviorally salient during maintenance?”

Patterns visible in v0.1

Self-monitoring + feedback recur
Tracking technology varies, but monitoring-linked feedback is the common backbone.
More contact is not reliably more value
Extra SMS, social comparison, or prolonged app exposure did not consistently add benefit.
Acquisition ≠ maintenance
Several early differences diminished or disappeared at later time points.
Relative advantage ≠ absolute increase
A group can perform better because it declines less.
Behavior ≠ physiological capacity
Higher walking or leisure PA does not guarantee higher fitness.
Packages create attribution problems
Multi-component trials often cannot isolate which component caused the effect.

Method boundary

v0.1 codes: population × target behavior × adherence problem × intervention × mechanism/component × duration × outcome × result. Mechanism labels stay descriptive; BCTTv1 numeric codes are not inferred. Null results are retained, and time point / absolute direction / between-group contrast are kept distinct.

Candidate v0.2 question: Under what conditions does a digital self-monitoring + feedback loop remain behaviorally salient long enough to support physical-activity maintenance?
